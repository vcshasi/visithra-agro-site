01 Our story

Good trading begins at the source.

Visithra Agro Traders is a family-led agricultural trading business from southern India, now in its second generation — built around honest relationships and products we are proud to stand behind.

Every product starts at the farm — hand-selected, carefully handled, and moved quickly to keep it fresh. We focus on direct sourcing, careful handling, and dependable communication, so quality agricultural products are always easy to source with confidence.

We've supplied a growing range of agricultural products to local businesses for years, and we have the capacity to expand our supplier network further as new partners come on board.

Gloriosa
Superba

A specialist botanical range supplied for regulated trade requirements. Gloriosa superba is recognised as a natural source of colchicine-related alkaloids used in pharmaceutical research and processing; regulated colchicine medicines are prescribed for conditions including gout.

Trade supply only — not for direct consumption or self-medication.
