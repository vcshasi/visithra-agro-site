# v1.3.5 brief

**Requested:** "when I scroll to the Products sections, the complete section is not visible, but rather the [heading] text at the top occupies most of the screen and the products pane is pushed down... Ultimate goal is navigate to product section -> display complete product section in the screen for the user (rather than the user to scroll up and down to view himself)." The user suggested shortening the heading text as one idea, but left the approach to me.

**Investigation:** measured the actual vertical stack on landing at `#products` at a typical 1280×800 viewport: header (76px) + section top padding (up to 92px) + heading block (h2 at up to 4.8rem across 2 lines + intro paragraph, ~250-300px) + index label margins (~50px) + the product card itself — which, after v1.3.3's fix giving Gloriosa Superba's gallery a taller frame (up to 700px) for the edge-to-edge photo fix, could reach ~850-900px tall on its own. Total stack easily exceeded 1400px, far more than any realistic viewport height — confirming the reported issue, and that my own v1.3.3 change had made it measurably worse.

**Delivered — five changes, verified together by measuring the actual rendered card position at several viewport heights (not just eyeballing):**
1. `.products h2` split from the shared `.about h2, .products h2` rule into its own much smaller size (`clamp(1.9rem, 3vw, 3.1rem)` vs. the original `clamp(2.6rem, 4.4vw, 4.8rem)`).
2. `.products` given its own smaller top padding (`clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)`) instead of sharing the generic `.section-padded` value; heading paragraph and `.product-index-label` margins tightened.
3. Gallery frame heights (both `.gallery-stage` and `.gloriosa-card .gallery-stage`, desktop and mobile) changed from a pure viewport-*width*-based `clamp()` to `clamp(min, min(vw, svh), max)` — capped by viewport *height* too, so the frame can't grow taller than the screen has room for.
4. `.product-card` padding reduced (2.4rem → 1.4rem), and the text-detail column tightened (heading margins, paragraph line-height/size, safety-note padding, sub-product list spacing) so it doesn't become the new limiting factor once the gallery shrank.
5. Verified end-to-end with real `getBoundingClientRect()` measurements at 1280×800 (fits with room to spare), 1280×720 (fits exactly), and 1280×650 (small remaining overflow — an honest limit given the regulatory description text Gloriosa Superba needs, not something to fix by cutting that content).

**Released as:** v1.3.5 (patch — layout/spacing fix to make an existing section behave correctly, per `VERSIONING.md`).
