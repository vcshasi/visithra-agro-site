# v1.6.7 brief

**Requested:** a cropped, annotated mobile screenshot showing the Gloriosa card mid-view with what looked like misplaced/overlapping content (a red box around the seam), captioned "Still not fixed" — a follow-up after v1.6.6's fix for the header Products submenu not landing correctly.

**Investigation:**
Tried hard to reproduce a *persistent* bug first, since that would be the more serious possibility: simulated the exact scenario of being on the Coconut card and clicking "Gloriosa Superba" from the header (the largest possible jump, most likely to expose any race), and separately forced all scroll calls to apply instantly so the settled end-state could be inspected deterministically rather than fighting this test browser's tendency to suspend real smooth-scroll animation while a tab isn't in the foreground. Every attempt landed correctly: right family, right first photo (`gloriosaScrollLeft: 0`, closest-slide distance `0`), right vertical position, no horizontal overflow. Couldn't produce a wrong *final* state.

That pointed at the transition itself, not the destination. The click handler fires three independent smooth-scroll animations at the same instant: the page's vertical scroll to `#products`, the family track's horizontal scroll to the chosen card, and that card's photo gallery resetting to its first image. Each one's own target is correct (confirmed above), but three simultaneous smooth-scroll animations sharing one fixed, `backdrop-filter`-blurred header is a plausible source of a torn or glitchy-looking frame while they're mid-flight, even without any of them being logically wrong — which would explain a real screenshot capturing something that looked broken despite the underlying state being fine.

**Delivered:**
Changed the header submenu handler to sequence the scroll instead of firing it all at once: the vertical scroll to `#products` goes first, and only once it has genuinely finished — detected via the `scrollend` event, with a 700ms `setTimeout` fallback for the rare browser without it — does the horizontal track scroll and the photo-gallery reset begin. `autoplayFamilyIndex`, clearing `autoplayStopped`, and calling `startAutoplay()` don't involve any visual motion, so those still happen immediately on click as before; only the two *moving* parts were deferred.

**Verified:** re-ran both the mobile (large Coconut→Gloriosa jump) and desktop scenarios with scroll forced instant — both still land correctly (family, photo, vertical position, no overflow) after the refactor. Confirmed `scrollend` is supported in this test browser and that the sequenced handoff actually fires and completes within about a second under real (non-instant) conditions. No console errors.

**Released as:** v1.6.7 (patch — a further fix to v1.6.6's own navigation work, per `VERSIONING.md`).
