# v1.5.0 brief

**Requested:** a screenshot showing the product-family pagination row cut off at the bottom of a very wide browser window — "Still the complete contents of the product screen doesn't fit the screen. Fix this." Explicit permission given to rearrange, move, or resize components and bring in ideas: "make this section more elegant and user friendly. Because this is the section users would be mostly interested in."

**Root cause, finally found:** every previous round of "fit to screen" work (v1.3.5, v1.4.2, v1.4.5) had trimmed text, padding, and margins, but never touched the actual photo frame's sizing formula. Since v1.4.0's switch to a uniform cropped-photo treatment, `.gallery-stage` used `aspect-ratio: 4/3` — a formula driven entirely by the frame's *width*, with zero awareness of the viewport's *height*. On a wide-but-short browser window (which the screenshot's very wide, apparently not-full-height window strongly suggested), the frame stayed at its width-computed size no matter how little vertical room remained. Previous verification passes had only checked two specific viewport sizes (1440×816, 1366×768) that happened to both be tall enough to mask this; a short-but-wide window like the user's was never actually tested.

**Delivered:**
- `.gallery-stage` height changed from `aspect-ratio: 4/3` to `height: min(40vw, 28svh)` (mobile: `min(65vw, 34svh)`) — now genuinely shrinks on short viewports, the same principle the hero section already uses for its own sizing.
- Redesigned `.subproduct-list` from bordered full-width rows to compact wrapped chips — shorter, and a cleaner look, which doubles as the "make it more elegant" ask for the section's most space-hungry element (especially Coconut's 4 items).
- Rebalanced `.product-card`'s columns (`1fr 1fr` → `.9fr 1.1fr`) and tightened remaining text spacing further, now that the image is properly compact.
- Verified against a real spread of viewport heights instead of two fixed numbers: comfortable fit (41-228px to spare) from 700px up through 900px+, only marginally short (9px) at an extreme 650px — a viewport height unlikely in practice and far more forgiving than the previous complete failure at ~750-800px on a wide window.

**Released as:** v1.5.0 (minor — fixes an architectural gap in responsive sizing, not just a value tweak, plus a visual redesign of an element, per `VERSIONING.md`).
