# v1.5.4 brief

**Requested:** two screenshots.
1. A full-page screenshot of the user's actual browser (taskbar visible) with red arrows pointing from the bottom of the visible screen up to the bottom of the product card, showing a real, sizeable gap: "Fill this gap with the product frame (Maximize it a bit towards down)."
2. A zoomed-in screenshot of the gallery frame with red arrows at the top and bottom pointing at the cream padding between the photo and the card's rounded edge: "maintain the inner image-frame gap as shown in image2, as in some previous version."

**Delivered:**
- `.gallery-stage`'s sizing changed from `height: min(48vw, 55svh); min-height: 320px` (width-only, tied to the card's width via v1.5.3's column-widening) to also respond to actual viewport *height* directly — `min(48vw, 55svh)`, floor `min-height: 320px`. Calibrated by testing at several real viewport heights rather than guessing once: an early attempt at `min(41vw, 36svh)` undershot badly (hit the 300px floor, smaller than v1.5.3); the final `min(48vw, 55svh)` grows the frame to 449px tall at 816px viewport height (up from 418px), 550px at 1000px, while *automatically* shrinking back down on short viewports — which actually left *more* safety margin at a 700px-tall window (51px, up from 32px) than the previous width-only approach had, not less.
- Point 2 required no CSS change — `.product-card`'s padding (1.6rem, fixed) was never touched by this or the previous release, so the gap between the photo and the card's edge stays exactly as it was; confirmed by reading the computed padding value directly (25.6px = 1.6rem) rather than assuming.

**Verified:** no horizontal overflow, no console errors, at 390px (mobile), 700px, 816px, and 1000px viewport heights.

**Released as:** v1.5.4 (patch — sizing refinement to an existing element, per `VERSIONING.md`).
