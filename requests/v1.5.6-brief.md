# v1.5.6 brief

**Requested:** two screenshots.
1. A full-page screenshot of the user's actual browser (taskbar visible), arrows pointing from the visible screen bottom up to the bottom of the product card: "Extend the height of the product group frame. I think class somewhere in 'product-card product-card-gallery gloriosa-card' needs to be changed."
2. A zoomed screenshot of the gallery frame with arrows at the top and bottom pointing at the cream padding between the photo and the card's edge: "Increase the margin between product-group frame and the image itself."

**Delivered:**
1. Traced the gap in image 1 to a real bug, not just a sizing-formula limit: `.products` overrode `padding-top` with a small `clamp(1.25rem, 2.4vw, 2rem)` but never overrode `padding-bottom`, so it was inheriting the generic `.section-padded` value (up to 5.75rem/92px) — confirmed via computed style (`padding-bottom: 92px` vs `padding-top: 32px` at a 1536×816 test). Added a matching `padding-bottom` override to `.products`, and used the reclaimed room (plus some of the general "fits one screen" budget) to also grow `.gallery-stage` itself, `min(48vw, 55svh)` → `min(48vw, 60svh)`, so the frame genuinely extends further down rather than just deleting empty space. Calibrated the new `60svh` value against the shortest window in the established test matrix (700px tall) to keep a real, non-knife-edge margin (29px) rather than overflowing — a single proportional formula can't close the gap completely at every window height without risking overflow on shorter ones, so some gap remains at taller windows by design, same tradeoff already accepted in v1.5.3/v1.5.4.
2. Increased `.product-card`'s padding from `1.6rem` to `2.1rem` (25.6px → 33.6px per side) on desktop, widening the cream margin around the photo as marked in image 2. Left the mobile override (`max-width: 980px`) at `1.6rem` — mobile has much less spare width/height to give up to padding.

**Verified:** re-measured `.gallery-stage` height and the gap below the card at 700, 816, 864, and 1000px viewport heights, plus mobile (375×812) — 700px height (the tightest case) still has 29px of real margin below the card; no horizontal overflow at any size; no overlap between the gallery and detail columns; subproduct list not clipped.

**Released as:** v1.5.6 (patch — sizing/spacing refinement to existing elements, per `VERSIONING.md`).
