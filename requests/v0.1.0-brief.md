# v0.1.0 — Initial website brief

Create a modern, responsive single-page website for Visithra Agro Traders, an agricultural product distribution business. Include a hamburger header, About Us, scroll-led product offering, contact form, address placeholder, and footer. The initial product families are Coconut and Gloriosa Superba with the listed sub-products. The site must be suitable for free hosting and provide an email-ready contact route.
