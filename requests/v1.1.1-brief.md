# v1.1.1 brief

**Requested:**
1. Reduce the size of the Contact section's heading/intro text block — it looked cluttered against the contact details below it.
2. Make the "Sourced with care ✦ supplied with confidence ✦ built for partnership" tagline a single line — it was too big and wrapping.
3. Reduce unnecessary gaps between sections — some spacing was more than needed, though gaps themselves are wanted.
4. Change the hero headline "Rooted in care. Ready for trade." to something similar but not identical (example given: "Rooted with care. Ready for Trade"), and reduce the hero text block size so it's proportionate with other fields — it read as too big.
5. Enhance the copy with more compelling, agro/farm-business language — e.g. products sourced direct from the farm, freshness, quality.

**Delivered:**
- `.contact h2` now has its own smaller `clamp()` separate from the About/Products headings; contact intro paragraph and the contact-details block were both tightened (smaller font, reduced margins/padding).
- `.supply-line` font size reduced substantially and set to `white-space: nowrap` above the 760px breakpoint (reverts to normal wrapping on small phones, where forcing one line would either overflow or need illegibly small text).
- `.section-padded`, `.about-grid` bottom margin, `.hero-copy` padding, and the `.supply` band height were all reduced for a tighter (but still present) rhythm between sections.
- Hero headline changed to "Rooted with care. Ready to trade."; hero `h1` size reduced and now shares its maximum size with the About/Products `h2`s for visual consistency.
- Rewrote the hero intro, the second About paragraph, and two of the three principle-card descriptions to mention direct-from-farm sourcing, freshness, and quality, without changing the three-card structure.

**Released as:** v1.1.1 (patch — content edits and visual refinements to existing sections, per `VERSIONING.md`).
