# v1.3.6 brief

**Requested (three points, with two reference screenshots):**
1. "Both the gloriosa and coconut image frame looks broken now... the attached photo of coconut is the one I'm expecting. But it looks broken as well now" — the reference photo was the same Coconut example from before (image centered, space above/below), now reportedly not matching either gallery anymore.
2. "The product section resize is Ok. But... the complete product frame became too small. My suggestion is to leave the size of the product frame as before But to reduce the 'Naturally versatile. Carefully supplied.' text to fit in a single line... Reduce this space, but keep the product frame more in focus."
3. A second screenshot showed the Gloriosa gallery with the outer product-family prev arrow (←) visibly overlapping the inner per-photo prev chevron (‹) in the same spot, red-boxed: "scroll buttons... look cluttered. Place it any other position."

**Root cause for #1:** v1.3.5 added a viewport-*height* cap (`min(vw, svh)`) to the gallery frame heights, to solve the previous "section doesn't fit the screen" complaint. That directly conflicted with v1.3.3's fix, which relied on the frame being *tall enough* (relative to its width) that portrait photos become width-constrained by `object-fit: contain`'s own math, rather than height-constrained (pillarboxed). Both v1.3.3 and v1.3.5 were fighting over the same `height` value for opposite reasons — v1.3.5 won, shrinking the frame back below the threshold needed, so photos in both galleries went back to pillarboxing.

**Root cause for #3:** v1.3.4 positioned the outer carousel buttons at the product-card's own left/right edges. Once v1.3.5 also shrank the card's padding, the outer button's footprint started overlapping the inner gallery-nav button's footprint (both sit in "left-middle of card" territory at those pixel ranges) — confirmed by the screenshot.

**Delivered:**
1. Reverted gallery-stage heights (`.gallery-stage`, `.gloriosa-card .gallery-stage`, desktop and mobile) and `.product-card` padding/text-detail spacing (h3, description paragraph, safety-note, sub-product list) to their exact v1.3.4 values — no more `svh` capping. Re-verified against every photo's real natural dimensions: all 8 (5 Gloriosa Superba + 3 Coconut) now render width-constrained again.
2. `.products h2` given a much smaller, single-line size (`clamp(1.25rem, 1.9vw, 1.9rem)`, `white-space: nowrap` above 760px, `normal` below it) and the `<br />` forcing the old 2-line break was removed from the markup. The heading's grid column was widened (`.7fr 1.25fr .8fr` → `.55fr 1.5fr .7fr`) to fit it. This is the space saved — not the product card.
3. Moved the prev/next buttons out of the card's edges entirely, into a new `.product-pagination` row (prev arrow, "01/02" index, next arrow) centered below the whole card — no shared screen space with the inner per-photo gallery-nav buttons under any card height. No JS changes needed (still `.carousel-button` + `data-direction`).

**Net effect measured at 1280×900:** the product card is full v1.3.4 size (777px tall) and starts at y≈171 (down from y≈600+ before any of this work), a page well up from where it started, with the photos correctly edge-to-edge and no button overlap. A small amount of scroll remains to see the very bottom of the tallest (Gloriosa Superba) card — an explicit, accepted trade-off per point 2 above, favouring the product frame's correctness and size over a total zero-scroll fit.

**Released as:** v1.3.6 (patch — reverts a regression and repositions a control, per `VERSIONING.md`).
