# v1.6.1 brief

**Requested:**
1. Header: (a) highlight the currently-viewed section's nav link, (b) fix the Products dropdown collapsing when the mouse moves from "Products" into the list — so a subitem can actually be clicked/navigated to, (c) "More products Yet to come" should not be all-uppercase.
2. Product section: (a) prev/next buttons should wrap around instead of stopping at the ends, (b) autoplay should stop on any click anywhere on the site, not just within the Products section, (c) make the photo nav buttons glow like the family nav buttons — keep both uniform in look and wraparound behaviour, (d) keep "Gloriosa Superba" on a single line too.
3. Include the © symbol where necessary, e.g. in the logo.

**Delivered:**
1. (a) Added a scrollspy: `IntersectionObserver`s on `#home`/`#about`/`#products`/`#contact` with a `rootMargin` biased to a thin band near the viewport's vertical centre mark whichever section is "current" with an `.active` class on its nav link (rust colour + permanent underline).
   (b) Root-caused the dropdown bug: the 14px gap between "Products" and `.submenu` fell outside both elements' hoverable boxes (since `.submenu`'s `position: absolute` box only started *after* the gap), so `:hover` broke while crossing it. Restructured into `.nav-item > .submenu-wrap > .submenu`: the wrapper starts flush against the trigger (`top: 100%`, no gap) and is sized to exactly match the visible card, with the gap now living inside it as padding rather than as dead space outside it — confirmed via `getBoundingClientRect()` that the wrapper's box is contiguous with the trigger and covers the full card footprint, and via a live hover-then-hover-into-list test that the dropdown stays open.
   (c) Removed `text-transform: uppercase` from `.submenu-note`.
2. (a) Both `.carousel-button` (family switch) and `.gallery-nav` (photo prev/next) click handlers changed from `Math.min`/`Math.max` clamping to modulo arithmetic, wrapping to the opposite end instead of stopping.
   (b) Added `document.addEventListener('click', stopAutoplay)` alongside the existing carousel-specific listeners, so a click anywhere on the page — not just inside Products — permanently stops autoplay.
   (c) Merged `.carousel-button` and `.gallery-nav`'s styling into one shared rust-glow treatment (border, fill, box-shadow glow, lime hover swap) and unified their size to 34px (38px on mobile), while each keeps its own positioning (`.gallery-nav` stays absolutely centred vertically inside the photo frame).
   (d) `<h3>Gloriosa<br />Superba</h3>` → `<h3>Gloriosa Superba</h3>`.
3. Added `<span class="brand-copy">&copy;</span>` next to "AGRO" in the brand wordmark, shared by the header and footer logos (same `.brand` markup). Caught a knock-on bug this introduced: the existing `.brand > span:last-child span` descendant selector also matched the new nested © span, forcing it `display: block` onto its own line below "AGRO" — fixed by narrowing both occurrences of that selector (base + footer colour override) to a direct-child combinator (`.brand > span:last-child > span`) so only the "AGRO" label itself is affected.

**Verified:**
- Scrollspy: "Home" active by default on load; scrolling to `#products` deactivates "Home" and activates "Products".
- Dropdown fix: `getBoundingClientRect()` confirms zero gap between the trigger and `.submenu-wrap`, and that the wrapper's width/position exactly matches `.submenu`; a hover-then-hover-into-the-list sequence (batched in one round trip, since this test browser's synthetic hover state doesn't reliably survive separate calls) shows the dropdown still `opacity: 1` / `visibility: visible` after moving onto "Gloriosa Superba".
- Wraparound: clicking "prev" on the family switcher while already on the first family (Gloriosa) correctly moves to the last (Coconut); clicking "prev" on the gloriosa gallery at photo 1 correctly wraps to photo 5 of 5 (confirmed via `closestGallerySlideIndex`, independent of the display label's own event-driven update lag).
- Global stop-on-click: dispatched a click on the hero heading (unrelated to Products) and confirmed `autoplayStopped` flips from `false` to `true`.
- Uniform buttons: computed `border-color`/`background-color`/size are identical between `.carousel-button` and `.gallery-nav` (both `rgba(217,92,49,.65)` border, `rgba(217,92,49,.2)` fill, 34×34px).
- "Gloriosa Superba" and the submenu note text: confirmed single line (rendered height ≈ one line-height) and `text-transform: none` respectively.
- © mark: confirmed present, inline (not block), and on the same line as "AGRO" after the selector fix.
- Mobile (375×812): full-viewport fullscreen nav still fills the screen correctly (per the v1.6.0 backdrop-filter fix), no horizontal overflow, all seven nav rows present. No console errors throughout.

**Released as:** v1.6.1 (patch — fixes and refinements to the v1.6.0 header/Products work, plus one small addition (scrollspy) in the same spirit, per `VERSIONING.md`).
