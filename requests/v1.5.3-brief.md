**Delivered:**

Used that budget to grow the actual photo frame: `.product-card`'s `min-width` cap raised from 1140px to 1200px — both grid columns get wider (still an even `1fr 1fr` split), so the frame grows via its existing `aspect-ratio: 4/3` (527×395 → 557×418 px at a 1366px-wide test), and the text column gets a little more breathing room too (tends to wrap to fewer lines, which itself frees up more headroom).

**Verified:** re-measured against the same spread of viewport heights used in v1.5.2 — 32px to spare at 700px (down from 52px, as expected/intended since the frame grew), 146px+ at 816px and up. Still a genuine margin at every size tested, not a knife-edge fit. No horizontal overflow, no console errors, mobile confirmed buttons still sit on the same line as the label.
