**Delivered:**
1. `.products-heading` changed from a centered flex *column* (v1.4.0) to a centered flex *row* (`align-items: baseline`), so the label and heading sit side by side again like the very first version of this section, just without the removed paragraph's third column. `.products .section-label`'s own styling is untouched.

2.2. Moved `.carousel-button-prev`/`.carousel-button-next` from the centered row below the card back to `position: absolute` on the card's left/right edges (`top: 50%`), same click behaviour as always (`data-direction` + generic `.carousel-button` selector in `script.js`, unchanged). Given this exact position previously caused an overlap with the inner gallery-nav buttons (the reason it was moved away last time), pushed the buttons further out this time (`-1.5rem` from the card edge instead of `-.5rem`) and verified the actual on-screen gap before shipping: ~22px clear at 1280px-wide desktop, ~10px on a 390px phone — no overlap at either size. The "01/02" index stays as a small centered label below the card (no buttons attached to it, so nothing to fight the inner buttons for room).
