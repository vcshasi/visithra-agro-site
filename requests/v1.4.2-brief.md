# v1.4.2 brief

**Requested:** the hero section ("Rooted with care. Ready to trade.") fits the screen perfectly when navigated to — no scrolling needed. Make Products and Contact behave the same way; currently both overflow and need a bit of scrolling to see everything.

**Investigation:** measured both sections directly against real viewport sizes rather than guessing.

- At 1440×816 (a common laptop size), Products' last visible content sat at y≈855 — 39px past the viewport. Contact's sat at y≈872 — 56px past.
- For Products, previous rounds had trimmed the heading and the gallery frame, but never noticed that `.products` (like every other section) was still inheriting the large, generic `.section-padded` *bottom* padding (92px at this viewport) — only the top had ever been overridden. Once the gallery frame became compact in v1.4.0 (a uniform 4:3 crop, ~390px tall), the product-detail *text* column became the tallest part of the card (518px) without anyone having trimmed it to match.
- For Contact, the same generic padding applied on both sides, and the enquiry form (7 fields + a 6-chip picker + a 4-row textarea + footer) had never been tightened at all this session.

**Delivered:**
- `.products` and `.contact` both given their own small `padding-top`/`padding-bottom` (`clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)`), replacing the shared large `.section-padded` value on both edges (previously only `.products`'s top had its own override).
- Products: tightened `.product-detail h3`/paragraph/`.safety-note`/`.subproduct-list` spacing again (this time with no image-sizing conflict to worry about, since v1.4.0's cover-crop approach doesn't care about the card's height), plus `.product-card` padding and the heading/index-label margins.
- Contact: tightened `.contact-form label`/input/textarea spacing, `.interest-field`, and `.form-status` margins; reduced the message textarea from 4 rows to 3 (it's still resizable — `resize: vertical` was already set).

**Verified, not assumed:** re-measured both sections at 1440×816 and 1366×768 after the changes — Products now ends with 88-136px to spare, Contact with 82-109px to spare, at both sizes, with no horizontal overflow and no console errors. Mobile was checked too: it still needs to scroll within these sections, same as it always has for the hero section itself once content stacks into one column — an expected trade-off of small-screen layouts, not something to fix by cutting content further.

**Released as:** v1.4.2 (patch — layout/spacing fix affecting existing sections, per `VERSIONING.md`).
