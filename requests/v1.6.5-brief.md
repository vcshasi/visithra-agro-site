# v1.6.5 brief

**Requested:** four screenshots with problem areas circled in red, from testing v1.6.4 on the user's own device: (1) the mobile Products submenu with "Gloriosa Superba" / "Coconut Products" / "more products yet to come" all missing their first few characters, clipped off the left edge; (2) a red box around the Coconut card spanning from the image down past the caption into the space above "02 — PRODUCT FAMILY"; (3) a red box around empty space to the right/below the Gloriosa card's text content, past the subproduct list and "Ask about Gloriosa Superba" link; (4) the same mobile submenu clipping issue again, in a narrower window. "Marked the areas of issues. Fix them."

**Delivered:**
1. **Mobile submenu clipping (screenshots 1 & 4):** `.submenu-wrap`'s mobile override (added in v1.6.0) reset `position`, `padding-top`, `opacity`, `visibility`, and `pointer-events`, but not `transform` — so the base rule's `transform: translateX(-50%)` (used to horizontally center the dropdown under "Products" on desktop) kept applying even under `position: static`, since CSS transforms aren't gated by positioning scheme. That shifted the entire mobile submenu block left by half its own rendered width, clipping the leading characters of every item off the left edge of the screen. Added `transform: none` to the mobile override.
2. **Empty gap in the product text column (screenshots 2 & 3):** traced this to a direct side effect of v1.6.4's own square-cap fix — now that `.gallery-stage` can grow as tall as it is wide, it's frequently taller than the text/detail column next to it, and `.product-card`'s `align-items: center` was centering the shorter column inside that taller grid row, splitting the leftover space into two disconnected-looking gaps (one above the heading, one below the "Ask about…" link) — exactly what both screenshots show. Fixed in two steps: changed `align-items` from `center` to `start` (both columns now align to the card's top edge, matching normal image+caption composition, eliminating the gap *above* the text entirely), then made `.product-detail` stretch to the image's full height and laid it out as a flex column with the "Ask about…" link pinned to the bottom via `margin-top: auto` — so on cards where the image is taller than the text needs, the remaining space now reads as an intentional "content up top, action anchored at the bottom" composition rather than a plain dead gap.

**Verified:**
- Mobile (375px, 700px-ish windows matching the reported screenshots): confirmed via `getBoundingClientRect()` (with the menu's open/close CSS transition disabled during measurement, since it otherwise mid-animates) that `.submenu-wrap` now sits at `left: 30px` — inside the viewport, matching the nav's own padding — instead of off-screen.
- Text-column gap: confirmed `.product-detail` now matches `.product-gallery`'s height exactly (both 540px at 1280×900, both 548.8px at 1920×1080) and the "Ask about…" link sits flush with the bottom of both, for both the Gloriosa and Coconut cards (which have different amounts of subproduct-list content).
- Re-confirmed the v1.6.4 fixes are untouched: gallery frame still a clean 1:1 square at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440, no horizontal overflow.
- Checked the single-column mobile/tablet layout (700px wide): the new stretch/flex rules are a no-op there (each card is its own grid row, so there's no taller sibling to stretch against) — the "Ask about…" link sits right after the subproduct list with its normal small margin, no injected gap.
- No console errors.

**Released as:** v1.6.5 (patch — bug fixes to v1.6.0's submenu structure and v1.6.4's own responsive fix, per `VERSIONING.md`).
