# v1.3.3 brief

**Requested:** "make portrait photos in gloriosa superba image frames also edge-to-edge with spaces in top and bottom similar to coconut" — v1.3.2 fixed the frame from shrinking to a narrow sliver around portrait photos, but the photos themselves still pillarboxed (empty space on the *sides*) inside that full-width frame, rather than filling it edge-to-edge like Coconut's landscape photos do (empty space top/bottom instead).

**Why this needed a frame change, not an image-CSS change:** with `object-fit: contain`, whether a photo ends up constrained by width (edge-to-edge sides, letterbox top/bottom) or by height (edge-to-edge top/bottom, letterbox sides — the "sliver" look) is decided purely by comparing the *frame's* own aspect ratio to the photo's aspect ratio — not by anything set on the `<img>` itself. Coconut's frame (~511×560, aspect ≈0.91) is wide enough, relative to its landscape photos (~4:3, aspect ≈1.33), that its photos are always width-constrained. Gloriosa Superba's frame was the same shape, but several of its photos are portrait (~0.75 aspect) — narrower than the frame itself — so they were always height-constrained instead. Verified this with the actual photo dimensions and the fit-vs-frame math before making the change, rather than guessing.

**Delivered:** Gave `.gloriosa-card .gallery-stage` its own taller height (`clamp(340px, 64vw, 700px)` desktop, `clamp(280px, 84vw, 480px)` mobile) instead of sharing Coconut's shorter one — tall enough that even the most portrait photo in the gallery becomes width-constrained. Verified against real photo dimensions: all 5 Gloriosa Superba photos (including the one landscape tuber shot) now render width-constrained (edge-to-edge, letterboxed top/bottom only). Coconut's frame and photos are untouched.

**Released as:** v1.3.3 (patch — visual fix to an existing element, per `VERSIONING.md`).
